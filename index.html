Collective Trust Framework

Built by us, verified together

1. GPS Proximity Radar

Your live location is protected in real-time. If you step near a danger zone, an immediate red "Alert" triggers. In safe zones, you'll see "You are safe".

2. 3-Peer Safe Confirmation

A safe zone requires 3 confirmations from distinct GPS locations (>50m apart). Trust Guardians contribute double vote weight.

3. Instant Danger Broadcast

Red danger pins notify nearby users instantly, initiating an immediate 30-minute community alert countdown.

Community Guidelines & Trust Scoring

Rules, Trust Score Changes & Tiers

Learn how your actions increase or decrease your trust score and affect your standing in the community.

Actions That INCREASE Trust Score

  • Safe Zone Fully Verified (+5 pts): When a safe zone you submitted successfully receives 3 distinct confirmations.
  • Accurate Confirmations: Participating honestly in verifying community safety corridors.
  • Receiving "Thank You" messages: Demonstrates reliable, helpful community support.

Actions That DECREASE Trust Score & Ban

  • Report Spam (-10 pts & 30-min Ban): Creating more than 3 safe reports within 30 minutes triggers an automatic penalty.
  • Malicious False Reporting: Submitting fake hazard pins results in heavy trust deductions and possible restriction.
  • Spamming Confirmations: Trying to confirm from restricted or duplicate locations.
